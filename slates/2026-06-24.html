Prediction: Bosnia win, in an open game. Most likely 2–1; alternates 2–0, 3–1.

  • Stakes/tactics (decisive): because both need a win, expect an open, two-directional match rather than a Qatar low block — goals at both ends are in play. (scout-form / scout-tactics)

Players to watch: Edin Džeko (captain, focal point + set-piece target, after a "difficult outing" last time); Akram Afif (Qatar's main creative threat — their route to the goal they need).

What would flip it: Qatar scoring first and Bosnia (nervy, poor last out) failing to respond; or Bosnia's makeshift back line leaking early in an end-to-end game.

Most likely scorer: Youssef En-Nesyri as the likely focal striker (low confidence — striker not yet XI-confirmed; defer to lineup check). Alternates: Hakimi (overlap / set-piece / penalty) (low–medium — personnel lesson favours the full-back vs a deep block) and Brahim Díaz (low).

What would flip it: Morocco easing off the moment qualification is mathematically locked (Brazil leading in Miami), letting Haiti grab a historic morale point.

Confidence & stake: Low–medium. 1 unit on Brazil to win. Stake cut from v1's 2u: deep block plus the loss of Brazil's primary width carrier is a doubly-capped spot, and the draw is a live ~33%.
